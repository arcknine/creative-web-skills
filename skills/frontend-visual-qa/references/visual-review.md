# Visual review method

Inspect from the whole page toward details. Do not begin by adjusting pixels.

## First pass: five seconds

At the initial viewport, identify the brand, primary message, focal point, and action. Note where the eye lands first, second, and third. If all elements have equal weight, record a hierarchy failure rather than several cosmetic issues.

## Page pass

Scan the full page at thumbnail scale for density, color balance, section cadence, repeated structures, and abrupt dead zones. Then inspect each section at normal scale:

- Does one element lead?
- Do type sizes, weights, and measures match content priority?
- Do margins, baselines, and image edges align intentionally?
- Does imagery retain its subject and provide usable text contrast?
- Does the transition from the previous section change pace deliberately?
- Does decoration reinforce a motif or merely fill space?

## State pass

Inspect navigation open/closed, hover, keyboard focus, pressed/selected, form validation, loading, and content extremes. Check contrast in every layered state, not only the default screenshot.

## Evidence

Name the viewport and region for each issue. Describe the visible symptom, its consequence, and one bounded remedy. Rank by reach:

- **High impact:** identity, hero, core hierarchy, navigation, dominant crop, or a repeated systemic failure.
- **Medium impact:** section rhythm, secondary hierarchy, local composition, or a breakpoint weakness.
- **Polish:** optical alignment, small state refinement, isolated spacing, or final consistency.

Prefer one systemic change that resolves several symptoms. Recheck the whole page after applying it.
