# Micro-interactions

Use small interactions to confirm affordance and express the larger motion language.

## Links and buttons

Make hover and focus related but independently legible. Use underline movement, label exchange, controlled fill, icon displacement, or shape change tied to the brand. Keep the hit area stable and state transition near 120–250ms unless the concept demands otherwise.

Magnetic buttons should move only a few pixels, preserve the actual hit target, reset cleanly, and run only for precise pointing devices. They should never make a primary action harder to catch.

## Media and cards

Use crop shift, scale, caption reveal, or preview swap to communicate what opens. Avoid scaling every card and adding a shadow. Keep media within its mask and prevent hover-only information from becoming inaccessible on touch.

## Cursors

Custom cursors must add context—such as “view,” drag direction, or media state. Retain the system cursor for text, controls, and accessibility settings. Disable on coarse pointers and when performance suffers.

## Feedback

Inputs, toggles, accordions, menus, loading, success, and errors need immediate state acknowledgement. Animate geometry only when it explains continuity. Maintain focus visibility, announced state, and sufficient contrast throughout the transition.
